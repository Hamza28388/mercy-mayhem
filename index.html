🃏 CARD GUIDE

What each card does

⛔ SkipNext player loses their turn.
⇄ ReverseChanges the direction of play. With 2 players, it also skips the opponent.
+2 / +4 / +6 / +10Next player draws that many cards unless they can stack a permitted draw card.
⇄ +4 ReverseReverses direction and starts a 4-card draw penalty.
🗑 Discard AllDiscard every other card in your hand matching this card's color.
♟ Skip EveryoneEveryone else is skipped; you play again.
7 — SwapChoose another active player and exchange hands.
0 — PassAll active players pass their hands in the current direction.
🌈 Rainbow RouletteChoose a color. Each opponent draws until they reveal that color.
✦ WildChoose the color the next player must follow.
UNO!Call UNO when you have one card left. If caught before your next turn, draw 2.

StackingPlay a draw card of equal or higher value than the current accumulated penalty. Otherwise, draw the full penalty.
Draw cards+2, +4, +6 and +10 add to the penalty total. +6 and +10 are rarer wild cards.
Turn paceBot turns pause before playing so you can follow the action.
Mercy RuleReach 25 cards and you are eliminated immediately.
WinningBe the first to empty your hand, or be the last player remaining after eliminations.
